# Owl's Eye

A calm pixel-art hidden-object game starring Rohit, his owl and his black panther.

**Play:** open `index.html` in a browser (keep `hero.webp` next to it). No build step and no dependencies.

## Scenes

| Scene | Find |
| --- | --- |
| Whispering Forest | golden feather, acorn, old compass, lantern, blue butterfly, iron key, plus a carved rune and paw tracks (owl only) |
| Moonlit Ruins | lost crown, old scroll, ruby, sun medallion, hourglass, clay pot, plus a hidden eye glyph and moon sigil (owl only) |
| Sunset Shore | starfish, spiral shell, shy crab, message bottle, old anchor, my sunglasses, plus a hidden pearl and turtle tracks (owl only) |

## How to play

- **Click** an item when you spot it. The owl swoops in to collect it.
- **Owl's Eye** (`O`): a night-vision lens that follows your pointer. It reveals items marked **OWL** and makes normal items twinkle. It uses energy and recharges when off.
- **Panther Sniff** (`P`): the panther runs a paw-print trail to an unfound item. You get 3 per scene.
- Each scene gives 1–3 stars based on time, misses and sniffs used. Best stars are saved in your browser.
